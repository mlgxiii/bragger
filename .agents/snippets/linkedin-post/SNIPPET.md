Write a LinkedIn post of 120 to 220 words. Open with one line that asks a question
the reader cannot dismiss. Short paragraphs of one or two sentences, blank lines
between them. One idea per post. End with a question or one plain call to action,
never both. At most three hashtags, on the last line, or none. No emoji bullets.
