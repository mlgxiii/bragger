@title: SEO blog post
@output: out/seo-blog.md

Write a blog post of 700 to 1,000 words in Markdown. The H1 is the question people
type into a search engine. The first paragraph answers it in two sentences. Then
three to five H2 sections, each answering one follow-up question. End with a short
"Sources" list of every URL used. Add a line at the very top:
`description: <one sentence, 70 to 160 characters>`.
